Privacy

Short version. The app keeps your writing on your Mac and sends nothing anywhere. This website asks for your email before the download and counts visits, and that's all.

The app

Seyes stores your notes as plain files in a folder you choose. It has no account and no analytics, and it makes no network calls, except when you choose Check for Updates, which asks GitHub for the latest version number.

Your email

When you download Seyes from this site, we ask for your email. We keep it, with the website that sent you here and your browser's language, in a database only we can read, hosted by Supabase.

We use it for one thing, to tell you when there's a new version of Seyes. We don't sell it or share it. Every email we send has a one-click unsubscribe.

To have your email deleted, write to seyes.writing@gmail.com and it's gone.

Visits

We count visits with PostHog, hosted in the EU, to know how people find Seyes and how many download it. It sets no cookies and stores nothing on your device, and we don't record your screen or identify you.
